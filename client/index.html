    Rules of Pristine Pines

    • Host creates a room and shares the code.
    • Host picks Free-for-All or Team Deathmatch, kill limit and time limit.
    • Every kill is a point. First to the kill limit wins — or most kills when time runs out.
    • You respawn 3 seconds after being fined. Brief spawn protection ends when you shoot.
    • Leaf Blaster 3000: automatic. HOA Enforcer: brutal up close. Deer Rifle: right-click to scope, one headshot drops anyone.
    • Trampolines are load-bearing. Headshots count extra.
